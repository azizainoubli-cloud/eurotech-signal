                Data: Tech.eu Funding Explorer (licensed CC BY 4.0), combined with a monthly scan of national tech media in 10+ countries, startup and investor announcements and press wires. A scanned round joins the totals only when at least two independent sources confirm it and Tech.eu does not already list it, so nothing is counted twice. Publicly announced rounds of startups based in Europe since 2021; amounts converted to euros at the ECB rate on each round's date; undisclosed amounts are left out of totals, not estimated. Israel is excluded. Country views cover the EU27, the UK, Switzerland, Norway and Iceland.

                Exits come from Tech.eu's exits feed; unicorn valuations from Wikipedia's list of unicorn companies (CC BY-SA). Not covered: founder gender, business model and ownership percentages (cap tables of private companies are not public). Coverage is announced rounds only, so databases that also count unannounced rounds report more deals.
